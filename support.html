Frequently Asked Questions

What is Moolzen?

Moolzen is an educational, simulated stock trading platform operated by Moolzen LLP. It enables users to practice stock and commodity trading with ₹10,00,000 in virtual capital, complete micro-lessons, and compete on global leaderboards risk-free.

Does Moolzen involve real money trading or gambling?

No. All trading on Moolzen is strictly simulated using virtual currency. No real monetary deposits are traded in financial markets, and no real-money trading risks or financial advisory services are provided.

How do I request account and data deletion?

You can request full deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by visiting our Account Deletion page or by emailing contact@moolzen.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

How do subscriptions or premium purchases work?

If you subscribe to Moolzen Premium, payments are processed securely through certified payment gateways. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime via your account settings or by contacting our support team.
