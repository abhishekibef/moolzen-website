Account Deletion Request

We are sorry to see you go. If you would like to permanently delete your Moolzen account and all associated data, please follow the instructions below.

Warning: Deleting your account is permanent. All your data, preferences, and history will be permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

How to request deletion:

Please send an email to support@moolzen.com from the email address associated with your Moolzen account with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Our support team will process your request and delete all your data within 7-14 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been completely removed.
